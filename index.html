11×11 State Puzzle Solver

Paint the 121 cells into states. The game automatically checks connectivity, symmetry, capitols, travel costs, clue values, row sums, and the final score.

How to play

Left-click or drag to paint cells into the active state. Left-clicking a cell already in the active state removes it, so a selected square can be toggled off. Right-click also erases. Shift-click a filled cell to make its state active. Create a fresh state with N. Press M or use Mark capitol, then click a filled square to mark or unmark that state's capitol. Each state can have at most one marked capitol. Delete any state from the States panel. The small lower-right number is the state's current size.
